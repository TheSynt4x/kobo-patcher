Kobo Patcher

Patches your Kobo with its official firmware file. Everything runs in this browser tab, and nothing is uploaded.

This browser can't write to a Kobo. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1Connect your Kobo

Plug it in, tap Connect on the Kobo, then select the KOBOeReader drive. The page reads its model and firmware version from there.

2Patches

3Firmware file

4Patch

Other models and firmware are refused. Patching system files can leave a Kobo needing a factory reset, so use this at your own risk. A firmware update removes the patch, so patch again afterwards with the new firmware file. Community patches come from kobopatch-patches (MIT licence) and its contributors.

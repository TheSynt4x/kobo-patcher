Kobo Patcher

Patches your Kobo with its official firmware file. Everything runs in this browser tab, and nothing is uploaded.

This browser can't write to a Kobo. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1Connect your Kobo

Plug it in, tap Connect on the Kobo, then select the KOBOeReader drive. The page reads its model and firmware version from there.

2Patches

Add dictionaries (optional)

For languages Kobo has no dictionary of its own for, such as Russian. Choose dictionaries in Kobo's format that you've downloaded, named dicthtml- and the language, such as dicthtml-ru.zip. They go in the Kobo's .kobo/custom-dict folder and show in its dictionary settings as Custom. Kobo's own dictionaries are downloaded from its Settings instead.

3Firmware file

4Patch

Only works on the models and firmware listed under the ? button. A firmware update wipes your patches, so you'll need to patch again after one. Community patches are from kobopatch-patches. Not affiliated with Kobo. Source on GitHub.

Use at your own risk

This changes system files on your Kobo. If something goes wrong you might need a factory reset, and it could even brick your device.
